A beginner's observing log

Letting an AI drive the telescope

I'm new to astrophotography. In October 2026 I bought a Seestar S30 Pro smart telescope and, instead of learning every piece of software at once, asked Claude to help me drive it.

This is a hobbyist site. It records what happens when Claude, Anthropic's AI, writes the control software, measures what the garden can see, plans the night, points and focuses the telescope, takes the pictures and processes them. I set up the tripod and go to bed. Expect beginner mistakes, honest results and the occasional cloud.
