About this site

I'm new to astrophotography. In September 2026 I bought a Seestar S30 Pro smart telescope for astrophotography and, instead of learning every piece of software at once, asked Claude to help me drive it.

This is a hobbyist site. It records what happens when Claude, Anthropic's AI, writes the control software, measures what the garden can see, plans the night, points and focuses the telescope, takes the pictures and processes them. I set up the tripod and go to bed. Expect beginner mistakes, honest results and the occasional cloud.

Night log

  • 17 Oct 2026

    The Flaming Star, up close

    A polar alignment snatched through a gap in the cloud, the Flaming Star Nebula (IC 405) in a frame of its own, and two nights of the Rosette Nebula (NGC 2244) stacked together.

  • 16 Oct 2026

    Four nebulae in one clear night

    Dusk to dawn without a cloud: the Soul Nebula (IC 1848), the Flaming Star and Tadpoles, and the Rosette, and the polar alignment measured properly at last.

  • 13 Oct 2026

    Sunspots, after losing the Sun

    A first daytime session: the scope's compass sent it the wrong way, but once found, the Sun showed a sunspot group three times the width of Earth.

  • 11 Oct 2026

    The Elephant's Trunk, after a false start

    The scope switched itself off while I was out. Back home, two hours on the Elephant's Trunk Nebula (IC 1396) in Cepheus before the cloud came in.

  • 10 Oct 2026

    Three nebulas and a Kp 7 aurora

    Nearly four hours on the North America (NGC 7000), Heart (IC 1805) and California (NGC 1499) Nebulas between clouds, and an aurora too low to see from the garden.

  • 9 Oct 2026

    Four hours on the Veil, then the Pleiades

    First night on the EQ wedge: the Veil Nebula (NGC 6992) and the Pleiades (M45). Polar alignment under cloud, then clear skies all night.

  • 7 Oct 2026

    First night in the garden: Andromeda, the Veil and the Heart

    The first unattended night: the Andromeda Galaxy (M31), the Veil and the Heart, with pictures on my phone by morning.

  • 23 Sep 2026

    Day one: straight out of the box in the Lake District

    The Moon, the Fireworks Galaxy and the Crescent Nebula, using only the phone app. The "before" picture.
